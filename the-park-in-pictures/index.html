A year in the park

The Park in Pictures

Fifty photographs from around Bishop’s Park, gathered into five short walks. Tap any picture to see it full size.

Water & reflections

The river & the lake

The Thames runs the whole length of the park’s western edge, and within it lies a much-loved ornamental lake with its fountain and green bridge.

Borders, blossom & roses

Gardens in bloom

The old gardens of Pryor’s Bank, the formal beds, the urns and the spring blossom — a horticultural tradition tracing back to the botanic gardens of Fulham Palace.

Avenues & open ground

Trees & green walks

Veteran London planes, shaded avenues and open lawns — room to wander, to sit, and to let the meadow grass grow a little longer for the pollinators.

Courts, wheels & swings

Sport & play

The park is busy as well as beautiful — a skate park, ball courts and a playground that keep Fulham moving from morning to the last of the light.

People & volunteers

Park life & the Friends

The park is what we make it — our gardening volunteers, the walkers and picnickers, the photographers and families. This is the everyday life the Friends are here to look after.

Your park needs you

Every one of these views is worth protecting. Make local friends, keep active, support nature — and keep Bishop’s Park beautiful for all.
